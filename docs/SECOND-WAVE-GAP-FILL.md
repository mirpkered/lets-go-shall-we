# Second-Wave Gap-Fill Adventures

This expansion adds 51 distinct short adventures across 12 underrepresented story types. The law-and-witness category has four slots because it reuses the already-playable **What Did You See?**; it is intentionally not duplicated in the registry. This brings the scenario registry from 124 to 175. These summaries are an authoring index, not a replacement for the scenario source.

All new stories begin with a fresh traveler and can be understood without prior character history. Optional history, gear, or money changes specific choices; none is required to finish. Endings are authored as ordinary outcomes rather than numerical scores. The shared scenario helper gives each route a distinct forward-only beat and two short outcomes; no new carryable items are introduced.

## Design boundaries

- Medical and birth stories rely on family, neighbors, and qualified practitioners; the traveler does not diagnose or perform procedures.
- Messages, letters, legal papers, and private requests are handled without forced disclosure. The traveler states only what they actually observed.
- Work and trade choices use ordinary terms and do not ask the traveler to perform hazardous specialist tasks.
- Existing equipment has narrow, practical uses; this batch adds no carryable items.
- Danger is not added merely to make an adventure feel consequential. Quiet, uncertain, and uneventful outcomes are intentional.

## Classification matrix

This quick index makes the intended pressure legible across the batch. “Risk” describes physical danger, not whether a choice can disappoint someone. Social pressure is how much another person’s expectations weigh on the traveler. Mystery is whether the traveler is asked to resolve uncertain facts. Levels are qualitative: none, low, moderate, or central.
