## Access-gap decisions

The pre-expansion route-aware audit had only 3 Pocket Toolkit holders and 11 Foreman’s Multi-tool holders at 50 completions in the sampled pooled cohort. Their 22 and 24 compatible-use scenarios, respectively, made them the strongest source candidates. A rail-station transfer adds one Pocket Toolkit source; a mill repair-chest transfer adds one Multi-tool source. In the final comparable run these reached 38 and 49 holders, respectively.

The Compact Wheel Wrench has seven compatible-use scenarios and already has a cartwright context in **The Broken Axle**. A duplicate tool/repair-or-cash settlement was added there. It produced 12 selected/granted item awards in the pooled acquisition funnel but only nine holders reached the 50-adventure sample mark; this remains a small, low-realization cohort and is not a reason to add more wrench sources.

No new Supplies were added: all three already have several physical sources, including fixed-stock merchants, while cross-adventure opportunity rates remain only 0–5.4%. More acquisition would increase unused inventory rather than complete continuity.
